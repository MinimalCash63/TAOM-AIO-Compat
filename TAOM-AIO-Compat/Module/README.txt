TAOM AIO Compatibility Patch v1.5.8

Fixes for other mods when they run with TAOM (Tales from the Age of Men) 2.0.39 and 2.0.40 on Bannerlord 1.5.7, including with the Co-op mod.

Each covered mod has one switch on this module's page in Mod Options (MCM). A switch does nothing unless its mod is ticked in the launcher. Changing a switch needs a restart of the game (the Limitless battle size choice is the one setting that applies from the next battle without a restart). Off means off: every part of that switch is removed. In Co-op, every player should use the same switches.

This module edits no file of any other mod. It changes their behaviour only in memory while the game runs. It includes code from the TAOM team's compatibility patches and one troop file from Dual Wield - TAOM, both used with their authors' permission (see the list below); it includes no files of the main mods themselves.

What each switch does, and what to do if you turn it off:
- Retinues: lets Retinues v2 find the Harmony and UIExtenderEx libraries bundled in TAOM.Dependencies, and shows its Troops button on TAOM's map bar. If you turn it off, untick Retinues too, or Retinues v2 crashes the game at start-up. On a Co-op client Retinues builds and changes no troops locally (the host owns them; a joining player's clan should get no retinue of its own, see Known issues). During new-game set-up it waits with AI clan retinues until it is ready.
- RBM: TAOM's RBM balance for TAOM items and arrows (including the Harad quiver and, in TAOM 2.0.40, Legolas's ability arrows), weapon descriptions for TAOM's placeholder weapons, TAOM recruit and grain prices, RBM tournaments with TAOM troops and prizes (the same prize pick for every Co-op player), a guard so RBM's mercenary companies cannot stop the campaign tick on a Co-op client, crash guards on RBM's battle code (they can hide real RBM errors; each is logged once), and a readable RBM Changelog window. Copied from the TAOM team's RBM patch (RBM-TAOM-2.0.29.5): untick that module, because while it is ticked only the Changelog fix runs here. If you turn the switch off, RBM runs without TAOM's balance (TAOM arrows do almost no damage with RBM combat on); no crash is known from that. TAOM's tournament prize choice only offers prizes that are kept. In Co-op, RBM's caravans and mercenary companies are left to the host.
- Hold the Line: brings back its Depth morale feature under TAOM, in field battles only (as Hold the Line intends). Safe to turn off.
- Bodyguards: stops a crash in towns, castles and villages on Bannerlord 1.5.7 (settlement and hideout escorts cannot appear on 1.5.7). If you turn it off, untick Bodyguards too. In hideouts, and in battles where you are not the general, escort members who are not already on the field are not added; Bodyguards' repeated error there is gone.
- TAOM Voices: lets TAOM Voices run on Bannerlord 1.5.7 in single player. Its own build check was made for Bannerlord 1.4.8 and switched the whole mod off; this switch accepts the Bannerlord 1.5.7 game files it was checked with. In Co-op TAOM Voices stays off (restart the game between single player and Co-op). It also catches an error at campaign start so loading cannot repeat forever. If you turn it off, voices are off again; untick TAOM_Voices too, to be safe.
- Xorberax's Legacy: TAOM's trolls, mumakil and Sauron keep TAOM's own follow-through after a hit instead of Xorberax's cut-through value. Safe to turn off. TAOM's trolls, mumakil, war elephants, spiders and Sauron are also left out of Xorberax's Deadly Horse Charge and Deadly Combat. Deadly Combat no longer kills a troop that is already down, or one the game keeps from dying (conversations, cut scenes), and a bleeding troop that dies another way is not killed a second time. In Co-op, Deadly Combat only acts on troops the local machine owns, and the bank is not available to joining players (the host keeps it).
- Improved Garrisons: TAOM's party size, speed, food and clan finance rules stay in charge, with Improved Garrisons' garrison costs added on top. Improved Garrisons 4.3.1.0 and newer fix their own recruitment, map banners and upgrade-path dialog for Bannerlord 1.5.7; with the old 4.2.0.7 this switch repairs recruitment and map banners, and its "specify upgrade path" dialog stays unavailable. If you turn it off, untick Improved Garrisons too: with 4.3.1.0 its own finance and (by default) party size models then replace TAOM's, and its speed and food models too if switched on in its options; with 4.2.0.7 it can crash the game on two of its buttons. In Co-op its garrison costs are not charged on days Co-op blocks gold changes, and its hourly and daily routines do not run on a joining player's machine: the host's Improved Garrisons decides.
- Dual Wield: replaces the separate Dual Wield - TAOM module. Dual Wield's dual-wielding TAOM troops use the corrected troop file (the Harad Twin-Scimitar and the Umbar Corsair Twin-Blade wear armour that exists in TAOM 2.0.39 and 2.0.40); Dual Wield's own "NPC Dual-Wield Troops" option still decides whether they exist. Harad's Twin-Scimitar is reached from the Harad Footman (harad_reg_footman), because TAOM no longer has its old base troop. Nothing to untick if you turn it off, but keep it on with TAOM. Do not untick Dual Wield in a campaign that already has these troops.
- RTS Camera Command System: it looks for RBM under an old module name and so treats RBM 4.8.x as not installed. With RBM ticked and RBM's AI module on, the Command System is told RBM is there: it then leaves attacks on a chosen enemy formation to RBM's AI and does not put a formation on hold when its target is gone. Does nothing without RBM. Safe to turn off. TAOM's logs show its Marshal's Word and Seat of Seeing staying off in every battle while RTS Camera is ticked; the Command System handles orders then, and this switch does not change that.
- Dismemberment Plus: men, female elves and female Nazgul are dismembered the way Dismemberment Plus does it. Male elves, male Nazgul, orcs, uruks, Uruk-hai, berserkers, goblins, Saruman and dwarves can lose their head, made from their own head, hair and helmet; their arms and legs stay on, because cut limbs would need new models for each race. The cut head shows the race's usual face, not that soldier's face, and there is no wound cap on the neck; a hood worn as a cape stays on the body. Trolls, Sauron and creatures are never cut. Its errors go to this module's log instead of a Windows message box, and its slow motion is off during Co-op battles. If you turn the switch off, untick DismembermentPlus too. Players do not have to match.
- Party Quality: while TAOM's Hidden Hosts hides a party or army ("Unknown party", or a masked troop count), Party Quality adds no leader name or quality rating to its tooltip. Safe to turn off. Players do not have to match.
- Limitless (single player only): Limitless raises the game's limit of 2040 soldiers and horses on the field. With BattleSizeResized ticked, this switch lets BattleSizeResized's first wave use Limitless' slots, and lets Limitless start even if it loads after BattleSizeResized. It also makes a crash at the start of a battle less likely when a first wave no longer fits Limitless' budget (for example after TAOM's mumakil crews spawn): about 1% of the slots are kept free when the first wave is sized, and if that is not enough the budget is raised for that one spawn, but only where there is room (a battle size below Limitless' own limit, or a siege below 2040); no soldier is held back. With Limitless' default settings in a field battle only the kept-free slots help. The setting "Limitless battle size" (2040, 3000, 4000, 6000 or Unlimited = all of Limitless' slots) decides how many slots a battle uses, never more than Limitless' own setting; it applies from the next battle. While Limitless is active, BattleSizeResized's "Mission Agent Limit" has no effect; if Limitless' Agent Slot Limit is 2040 or less, keep that setting at 2040. If you turn the switch off, Limitless still starts when it loads before BattleSizeResized, but every first wave keeps BattleSizeResized's 2040 limit and a crash at the start of a battle is more likely: untick Limitless too.
  Limitless and co-op: this switch does nothing during a co-op session, and Limitless is a single-player mod. Untick Limitless in the launcher before hosting or joining co-op: Co-op's module check requires every player to have the same ticked modules, and the host's co-op server process is started with the host's ticked modules, so it would load Limitless too.
  Please note: Limitless runs an unsigned program (Modules\Limitless\System\Limitless.Injector.exe) once per game start, when the main menu first opens. It loads Limitless.Patcher.dll into the running game and changes the game engine in memory (no game file on disk is changed). Antivirus tools may flag this. It is Limitless' own design, not part of this module; install Limitless only if you trust it.
- TAOM escape menu: TAOM's pause menu (Esc) has room for about 8 buttons. RBM, Retinues and Co-op add their own, so "Exit to Main Menu" was hidden below the list. With this switch the list grows to show every button when they do not fit, up to the free space in TAOM's frame (10 buttons); with even more, the list scrolls and its scrollbar is wider. Nothing changes when the buttons already fit; TAOM's files are not changed. Safe to turn off: then scroll the mouse wheel over the list to reach the last buttons. Players do not have to match.

Not patched, for information: under TAOM, More Formation Banners' BannerBearerInterval setting has no effect (TAOM's own banner-bearer rule decides, with at most six bearers per formation); its back flags work.

Requires: TAOM.Dependencies and TAOM. Uses the Harmony and MCM libraries bundled in TAOM.Dependencies.
Install:
1. Install TAOM as its page describes, and the mods you want to use from their own pages.
2. Install TAOM Dependency Patch (Nexus 13196). In the launcher, tick its four entries, Harmony, ButterLib, UIExtenderEx and Mod Configuration Menu, each named "(TAOM Dependencies Proxy)", right after TAOM Dependencies and above Native. Mods such as Retinues and Party Quality list those libraries as required modules; the proxies let the launcher accept them without editing any mod. Do not also turn on separate Harmony, ButterLib, UIExtenderEx or MCM modules.
3. Copy the TAOM-AIO-Compat folder into Bannerlord's Modules folder and tick "TAOM AIO Compatibility Patch" right after TAOM, above the mods it covers. In TAOM 2.0.40's own order, LOTRLOME_Armory and Dual Wield load before TAOM; that is fine. Limitless can go anywhere below this module, for example right before BattleSizeResized.
4. In Co-op, every player needs this module at the same version, the same four proxies and the same ticked mods (Co-op checks this when a player joins).

Single player with Co-op ticked: while the Coop module is ticked, TAOM uses the game's standard map bar instead of its own and some TAOM features stay off, even in single player. Co-op also adds its own characters to the save (its team lords and wanderers); a save made with Coop ticked and later loaded without it can get TAOM's "repaired troops" message. For single player, untick Coop and TAOM.CoopCompat, and keep separate saves for single player and co-op.

Start-up warnings: this module shows a short in-game message when Coop is ticked while you play single player (once per game start, on the campaign map), and when Limitless is ticked together with Coop (at the main menu, and again if a co-op session starts). They never show in Co-op's server process. Turn them off in Mod Options with "Show start-up warnings" (applies from the next start).

Known issues:
- Retinues: at every game start two of this module's eight Retinues guards do not install. On a joining Co-op player's machine Retinues may therefore still create a default retinue when the player joins; the errors are written to Coop_client.log.
- This module does not compare its switches between Co-op players. Use the same switches on every machine.
- Bodyguards' settlement and hideout escorts do not appear on Bannerlord 1.5.7 (see above).
- TAOM Voices: in single player the voices do not play in conversations. The game does not crash. The cause is not known yet.
- Under TAOM, More Formation Banners' BannerBearerInterval setting has no effect (see above).

Log: Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\Logs\taom_aio_compat.log
To remove: untick it in the launcher (see the warnings above for mods that then need unticking too).
